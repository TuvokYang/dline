---
title: Trajectory Navigation
description: Preview user messages, inspect request-round and tool counts, and jump to earlier positions using the rail beside the chat.
---

Trajectory navigation is the rail of short bars on the right side of the chat. It locates earlier user messages in long tasks.

## Show the trajectory

1. Open a task with an existing conversation and move the pointer over the chat messages; no scrolling is needed.
2. The rail appears on the right. Each bar represents one user-message turn, grouped under **Attempt** bands.
3. Hover over a bar to preview the turn, then click it to jump to the corresponding user message.

The turn at your current reading position is highlighted. The rail and available scroll-to-top/bottom button stay visible while you hover over the message area or use its floating controls with the keyboard. They fade when the pointer leaves and no control has keyboard focus. You can focus a marker and press `Enter` or Space to jump.

The rail leaves room for the buttons below it. In a short message area, the buttons sit side by side; long trajectories scroll inside the rail so every turn remains reachable.

## What the preview contains

- **Attempt / Turn**: the group and turn numbers;
- a preview of that turn's user message;
- the recorded number of model request rounds;
- tool names and their call counts.
